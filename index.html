FAQ

Common Questions

How fast can I get approved for business funding?

Most clients who qualify get access to capital starting at $50,000 on the low end — approved and funded in less than two days from signing the service agreement.

What documents do I need to get started?

A picture of your government-issued ID, a picture of your Social Security card, and one proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or recent pay stub).

Do I need perfect credit to qualify?

No. Most clients start with high utilization or existing debt. The process restructures your profile first, then unlocks larger approvals as it strengthens.
