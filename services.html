Questions

Which Lane Is Right For You?

Which service should I start with if I need funding fast?

Start with Business Funding. It's the done-for-you application sequence across our lender network, with approvals starting at $50,000 and most clients funded in under 48 hours once documents are submitted.

What if my credit isn't strong enough to qualify yet?

That's what Rich Off Credit 2.0 is for. We dispute inaccurate negative items and use a personal-loan-to-business-credit strategy to lower utilization first, then move you into the funding sequence once your profile can hold the approval.

I'm brand new to this. Do I need to know anything before applying?

No. Funded Foundation is built for exactly that — the entry-point course that teaches you what to have in place before you ever submit an application, so you don't burn an inquiry or damage your profile chasing an approval you weren't ready for.

How is the Blueprint different from Funded Foundation?

Funded Foundation is self-paced fundamentals. The $100K Business Funding Blueprint is a live, multi-day cohort where Adreain walks through the entire framework in real time, including live approvals.

Can I build a business around this instead of just getting funded once?

Yes. The JKA Partnership Program is direct mentorship on the same marketing, webinar, and client-acquisition systems Adreain used to go from $0 to $84M+ funded, for people who want to build a funding business of their own.
